Guardrail
all 90 days		 Defer broad social-media expansion until Eden has the capacity, content pipeline, governance, and response structure to sustain it well. Awareness first; social later.
Success
looks like		 More people understand why Eden matters; more Black Philadelphians see Eden as part of their cultural inheritance; more institutions recognize a credible role for partnership; and awareness translates into the financial support needed to protect the cemetery for the next generation.
