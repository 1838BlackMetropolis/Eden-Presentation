The work, in one sentence

Eden protects more than graves. It protects the dignity, evidence, memory, and continuity of Black life, so that what was pushed out of sight is never pushed out of history.

Roughly 93,000 burials across 17,000 lots on 53 acres in Collingdale, Pennsylvania, founded in 1902. These deliverables are how that story gets told, walked, and kept.
