Partnership Pathway

Start small, build the relationship while you work, then decide together whether to go deeper.
PREPARED FOR 1838 BLACK METROPOLIS × HISTORIC EDEN CEMETERY  ·  2026
1
 Reach out
Open the door

Connect with a partner whose mission already touches Eden — a Black church, Divine Nine chapter, school, HBCU alumni network, neighborhood association, genealogical group, or cultural institution. Lead with the story, not the ask.

 Eden's move
  • One warm introduction or direct note
  • Share the "Why Eden Matters" page
  • Ask for a conversation, not a commitment
2
 Start small
Suggest one easy thing to do together

Propose a single, low-lift joint action with a clear date and a clear end — small enough that "yes" is easy, real enough that both sides learn something: a guided walking tour for their members, a family-history afternoon, a remembrance event, or a "That's ours" visit.

 Eden's move
  • Offer two or three options; let them pick
  • One event, one date
  • Agree on who does what — briefly, in writing
3
 Build the relationship
Develop trust while you do the work

The joint action is the relationship-building. Plan it together, show up for each other, and notice how the partnership actually feels — communication, follow-through, shared values, and whether their people connect with Eden.

 Eden's move
  • Co-plan, don't just host
  • Thank them publicly and personally
  • Hold a short, honest debrief
Decision point
Is this a partnership worth continuing?
Yes — go deeperShared values, good follow-through, and their people showed up. Move to Step 4.
Not yet — part warmlyThank them, keep the door open, and put your energy into the next partner. A small test that ends well is still a win.
4
 Deepen
Build a pathway to sustainable work together

Turn a one-time action into a repeatable one: an annual program, a recurring tour or heritage day, a community-ambassador role, or a sponsorship match to one of the four platforms. Name the partnership, put it on both calendars, and agree on what sustained support looks like.

 Eden's move
  • Propose a recurring rhythm
  • Invite them to become an ambassador
  • Match them to a sponsorship platform
Test small. Learn fast. Grow only the partnerships that prove themselves.
